Cold Plunge Therapy in El Paso

Sessions Only $15. No Membership Required — just walk in ready.

ReLife Recovery in West El Paso offers cold plunge therapy at a range of temperatures, so first-timers and experienced cold-plungers alike can find the right level of challenge.

What to Expect

Come dressed in swimwear or athletic wear you don't mind getting wet — a fitting room is available if you'd rather change once you're here. Our tubs are set at different temperatures, from 37°F, our coldest, up to 50°F for a less intense (but still genuinely cold) option — so you can choose based on your comfort level and experience. Staff will help you find the right tub and are always present during your session — for safety, to answer questions, and to help as much or as little as you'd like.

How Cold Is It?

Our tubs range from 37°F, our coldest setting, up to 50°F. Both are a real cold plunge — 50°F is simply less intense than 37°F, not warm. If it's your first time, starting at 50°F is a great way to build confidence before working toward colder temperatures on a future visit.

100% Chemical-Free

100% Chemical-Free

Our cold plunge water uses zero chlorine, bromine, or other chemical treatments — just clean, cold water. That means no burning eyes, no irritated or dried-out skin, and no chemical smell lingering on you after your session. It's a cleaner, more natural way to recover.

How Long Should You Stay In?

There's no single right answer — most people find 20 to 30 seconds is enough to feel the effects, while others stay in 5 minutes or more. Longer doesn't mean better recovery. Always trust what your body is telling you, and get out when it's time.

Staff Support, Your Way

Some people want quiet — just them, the cold, and their own focus. Others want a coach counting off 30-second marks and helping them breathe through the first plunge. Tell us which one you are, and we'll match it. Either way, staff is always present for safety and questions.

A Note on Safety: Please talk to a physician before your first session if you have a cardiovascular condition, are pregnant, or have uncontrolled high blood pressure — cold plunge may not be safe for these conditions.

Cold Plunge Questions, Answered

How cold is the cold plunge?

Our tubs range from 37°F, our coldest, up to 50°F — and the water is 100% chemical-free. You can choose based on your comfort level and experience.

How long should I stay in?

Most people find 20 to 30 seconds is enough, while others stay in 5 minutes or more. Longer doesn't mean better recovery — trust your body and get out when it feels right.

What should I wear?

Swimwear or athletic wear you don't mind getting wet. A fitting room is available if you'd rather change once you're here.

Is staff with me the whole time?

Staff is always present for safety and questions. Whether you want quiet focus or active coaching through the first plunge is completely up to you.

Is cold plunging safe for everyone?

It's safe for most healthy adults, but talk to a physician first if you have a cardiovascular condition, are pregnant, or have uncontrolled high blood pressure.

Do I need a membership to try it?

No — single sessions are $15, no commitment required.
