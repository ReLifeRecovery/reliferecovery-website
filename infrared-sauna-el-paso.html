Infrared Sauna

Infrared Sauna & Red Light Therapy in El Paso

Sessions Only $15. No Membership Required, just walk in ready.

Book This Session — $15

ReLife Recovery's infrared sauna pairs deep, penetrating heat with built-in red light therapy, so you get two recovery modalities in one visit.

What to Expect

You should expect to sweat, so wear anything you don't mind getting damp. Athletic wear or swimwear both work well, and shorts with a top are a common choice. A fitting room is available if you'd rather change once you're here. Staff will walk you through the settings before you start and stay present on-site throughout, for safety, to answer questions, and to help as much or as little as you'd like.

How Hot Is It?

Our infrared sauna can reach up to 149°F (65°C), though it's set to whatever feels comfortably warm for you. Infrared heat warms your body directly instead of just heating the air around you, so it can feel just as effective at a gentler setting than a traditional sauna.

Red Light Therapy Included

Red Light Therapy, Built In

Every session includes red light therapy built directly into the sauna panels, at no separate cost or appointment. Red light therapy, also known as photobiomodulation, has been studied for its potential to support skin appearance, circulation, and post-exercise muscle recovery. As with any therapy, results can vary from person to person.

How Long Should You Stay In?

There's no single right answer. Most people find 15 to 20 minutes is enough time for a beneficial session, though it's completely fine to step out sooner or stay a little longer based on how you feel. Trust what your body is telling you, and get out when it's time.

Staff Support, Your Way

Some people want to sit quietly and unwind. Others want help adjusting the heat or making the most of the red light. Tell us which one you are, and we'll match it. Staff is always present on-site for safety and questions.

A Note on Safety: Please talk to a physician before your first session if you are pregnant, have a cardiovascular condition, uncontrolled blood pressure, or are sensitive to heat.

Infrared Sauna Questions, Answered

How hot does the infrared sauna get?

It can reach up to 149°F (65°C), and is set to whatever feels comfortably warm for you.

How long should I stay in?

Most people find 15 to 20 minutes is enough for a beneficial session. It's fine to step out sooner or stay a little longer based on how you feel.

What should I wear?

Anything you don't mind getting damp. Athletic wear or swimwear both work, and a fitting room is available if you'd rather change once you're here.

Is red light therapy included?

Yes, every session includes built-in red light therapy at no extra cost.

Is staff with me during my session?

Is infrared sauna safe for everyone?

Can I do sauna and cold plunge the same visit?

How is infrared different from a traditional sauna?

Infrared heat warms your body directly rather than heating the air around you, so the room stays more comfortable while your core still reaches an effective sweat.
