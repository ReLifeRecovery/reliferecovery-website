Membership Questions, Answered

Is there a long-term contract?

No, both memberships are month-to-month. Cancel anytime.

Does my rate ever go up?

No, your founding rate stays locked in for as long as you're a member.

What's the difference between the two membership tiers?

Complete ReLife Recovery includes unlimited access to all three modalities for one member. The +Guest tier adds one guest per member per day.

Can I use single-session pricing instead of a membership?

Yes, single sessions are $15/visit, or $40 for the Full Circuit (all three modalities), no membership required.
